Mouse DPI test

Measure how many counts your mouse reports for a distance you choose, and get its DPI.

How to measure

  1. Lay a ruler on your desk and rest the mouse at the 0 mark.
  2. Pick the distance you will move it, then press Start. The cursor is captured so it cannot hit the screen edge.
  3. Slide the mouse in a straight line to that mark, then press Space or click to stop.

For an accurate result

  • Use a longer distance. Moving 4 inches (10 cm) is more accurate than 1 inch, because a small error in where you stop matters less.
  • Keep the movement straight and at a steady speed. Do two or three runs and compare them.
  • Turn off pointer acceleration (on Windows, untick “Enhance pointer precision”), because it changes how far the cursor moves.
  • This is an estimate. Browsers report movement in screen pixels, so display scaling and system pointer settings can shift the number. A result within about 5% of a common setting such as 800 or 1600 is a match.

Mouse and Keyboard Test

A free tool that checks every mouse button, the scroll wheel and every key on your keyboard, on your own computer.

Version 1.2.0 · November 2026

About the software

Press a button or a key and the matching part of the drawing lights up. Parts you have already tested keep a lighter tint, so you can see what is left to check. It is made for anyone who has just bought a mouse or keyboard, or suspects one is failing.

  • Mouse: left, right and middle buttons, plus the back and forward side buttons.
  • Scroll wheel: up and down arrows light up, with a count for each direction.
  • Keyboard: the full 104-key layout, with a running count of keys tested.
  • DPI: measures your mouse's DPI. Move it a distance you set with a ruler and the test counts how far the mouse reports moving.
  • Sound: plays a test tone through the left speaker, the right speaker or both, and shows a live level for any microphone you choose, with a short record and play back.
  • Problem notes: warns when a button or key registers two presses in under 60 ms, when the wheel suddenly reverses direction, and when nothing has been pressed for a few seconds.
  • Extras: a Reset button for each test and a light and dark mode.
